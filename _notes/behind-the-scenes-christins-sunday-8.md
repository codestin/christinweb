Dear Ones,

- 

One thing that’s been on my mind this week is the idea of resiliency. I’ve been researching this topic for the upcoming [workshop](https://lu.ma/yi52nszy) on Tues (you can still register btw!) and I reviewed an essay draft written by a psychologist friend on the same topic. 
- 

There’s of course the idea of Resiliency against hardships in life. The Big “R” Resiliency.
- 

But what I am hoping to build in addition, is little “r” resiliency as a creator.
- 

The resiliency to keep creating without measuring the ROI every 3 seconds, and not judge my own work or other’s work by how popular they are. To keep creating even when my schedules and to-do lists go haywire. When my energy/focus levels are low.
- 

I find that it’s much harder to share with my friends about little “r” because my projects sometimes don’t have clear financial goals.* But yet, I’m pretty sure I don’t want you guys to be celebrating my financial success at my funeral… 🤔 So refocusing back, **am I contributing to your life enough?****And****how are you feeling in terms of *****your***** little “r”?**

Warm Wishes,

Christin
