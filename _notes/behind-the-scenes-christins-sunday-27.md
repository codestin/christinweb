Christine and I got into the final round of the Maven course accelerator program, yay!
- 

We have one more step that we need your help with: can you please fill out this **[quick 5-question survey](https://forms.gle/TB1rpQuYfiFAQHnMA)**? I’m learning quickly that it takes a whole village to make a course, and I would like to ***give back as much as possible***. Would it help if I document the process of course-making? What would you like to learn, on a meta level?

**3 Bullets of Vulnerability:**

- 

I struggled with getting this newsletter out today. Between getting knocked off my feet with the vaccine booster, holiday socializing meetups, getting ready for a potential local move, amongst other reasons, I had a hard time putting fingers-to-keyboard.
- 

As I fell asleep last night I visualized the inner workings of my mind, and I saw that similar to [extremely dirty homes](https://www.youtube.com/c/Aurikatariina/videos), I’ve over the years accumulated a lot of unpleasantness within, and while I’m able to be “in my inner home” most days and function, extreme stress levels throw me off-kilter. Like living in a dirty home and getting used to it most days, and only realizing how terrible it is on certain high-stress days.
- 

So what does this all mean? This week, I’m working on restoring faith in myself—a form of tidying up I suppose—and working on making a nicer inner home. I aim to get back to my routine and get back to writing again! So I added a “Draft 0s” section to my [personal website](https://christinchong.com) as a placeholder.
