## State of the Union Address 🫡

My Fellow Americans Dear Friends,

I used to update a [/now](https://christinchong.com/now) page like [Derek Sivers](https://nownownow.com/about), listing my routine under 5 categories: Cultivation, Connection, Creativity, Constitution, and Currency. 

Such vain attempts at categorizing the messiness of being human must amuse the gods. But if somehow, I am making progress across these 5 categories, then the train is chugging along, and all is right in the world.

This newsletter replaced the /now page, yet it is usually a canary in a coal mine. When it stops chirping, it means *something* is amiss. As I loaded up my Substack page, I found myself going wait, what? I haven’t published in over 2 months?! Yet, the train is chugging along, and all *is* right in the world.

So let me share what I’ve been up to by reviving the ol’ format for the State of the Union address.

### Cultivation:

This category holds spiritual practice and matters of the heart. I’ve semi-joked that my spiritual “key performance indicators (KPIs)” are loving-kindness and letting go (here’s my **[video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=2KqxHR1IXII)** about the topic.) I had a discussion with my teacher Gil Fronsdal that confirmed positive signals on this front. (It’s always good to validate learnings with trusted teachers because it is easy to be led astray by our egos.) This has been the strangest thing—the subsequent categories are a lot messier, yet I am more at peace and settled.

In fact, *so* at peace that this newsletter fell by the way side! 😅 In disentangling my ego from accomplishments, I’ve taken to only doing 2-3 tasks per day, mostly towards building **[Human Intellect](https://humanintellect.co)** with . Perhaps this was a respite I needed and I do have renewed creative energy (discussed below), but I’m more certain than ever before about the benefits of practice.

**Where I am spending my time:**

**[Maggasekha](https://maggasekha.org/)**, **[Clear Mountain Monastery](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCazJbUOGWBpBVXU8Rv2gwsw)** and **[Buddhist Insights](https://buddhistinsights.org/#third)** online communities, Wednesday half-day retreats at **[Insight Meditation Center](http://insightmeditationcenter.org)**. All of these places offer helpful videos, and the monastics in the first 3 places often answer questions about practice on livestream.

### Connection:

In the past few months I had shifted my online presence to the Farcaster network, as an alternative to X/Twitter. I tried a few other places like Bluesky and Mastodon, but found that Farcaster has been the place that “clicked” the most. There is a particular magic to this place—the people are friendly and innovative (many are in web3), and I see delightful posts every time I check the platform. I’ve had Zooms, Telegram group chats, and IRL meetups with a number of mutuals.

In fact, some friends have helped me establish the **[world’s first onchain interfaith chaplaincy service](https://gan.gg/christin)**! I’ve held a few sessions and it’s been a great way to connect with new folks.
