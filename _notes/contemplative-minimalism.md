## What are our true material needs in life? 

This week I’m intrigued by this extreme minimalist person called Aki, who posts on a YouTube channel called [Samurai Matcha](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCR9ygnRBOt8qfSbkCwOA3pw). 

He challenged himself to start off with only his Samurai-style underwear ([fundoshi](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Fundoshi)), and allowed himself 1 additional item per day for 30 days. 

It’s an interesting exploration of the **minimum number of things we truly need to live a contented life.** I recently de-cluttered and enjoy the extra space in our tiny apartment, and this video challenged me to further examine my true essentials.
