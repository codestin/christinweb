## **3 Bullets of Vulnerability*:**

- 

Oh boy, this week has made me realized that perhaps building 3 businesses, 2 doing 2 cohort-based courses, and running 1 non-profit all simultaneously might have me strayed from my original goals. You might laugh but my original goals have always been to design a life where I can **1) go on longer meditation retreats and 2) have a peaceful ideal day where I get to take time to do deep work and spend quality time with Yuhong.** Oops! 

- 

There’s quite a lot to unpack here and warrants its own essay (ha!) but essentially, I can see that there’s **subconscious mistrust of whether my goals are even achievable**—I seem to model “success” from seeing how my family works (aka all the time), and don’t think that one can “get there” without being busy and exhausted all the time. 

- 

I also want to work on trusting that I won’t “slack off.” It’s almost like I entirely rely my internal drive to do things based on fear, instead of hope, you know? It’s “served me well” to get to where I am today, but I can’t imagine keeping this up for another decade…I’m exhausted just thinking about it! So this Thanksgiving I’ve given myself the task (ha!) of un-tasking myself. **Build a realistic ideal day that is enjoyable.**

Thanks for reading! **What’s your ideal day like? What’s in the way? Let me know! :)**
