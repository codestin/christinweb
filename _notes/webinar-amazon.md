- Consider using a pen name/pseudonym (Greg is a pseudonym) if you have varied interests/want to experiment in different genres
  - Greg has math books and Christian books under another name
- Possible to work at your own pace for these kinds of projects
- Don't worry about not being the expert: summarize content from existing sources
- Will Greg's strategy work for other genres? E.g. Hassan Osman and other authors have succeeded in the leadership/productivity category
- Use Kindle Direct Publishing
- Greg started in 2011 (11 years ago!)
- Unlike Gumroad (where you peak on launch and need to constantly maintain), Amazon has a longer tail
- Greg doesn't have social media and doesn't rely on audience building strategies outside of Amazon
  - Publish first or build an audience first? Publish first b/c this is not an audience dependent platform (Greg doesn't even know his readers!)
- Lifetime royalties: $178K
- Be aware of writing about trademarked information
- Greg has 2-3 books written by ghostwriters, but the issue is that when people ask questions you can't really support them and the quality is not that great, heavy editing work
- Be aware of royalty differences for different pricing ($2.99-$9.99 is what Amazon's pushing authors towards)

## Publishing Tips

**Use [KDP paperback / hardcover template](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201834230) and [cover template](https://kdp.amazon.com/en_US/help/topic/G201953020) (most common is 6" x 9")**
- **E.g. the margins are actually uneven on purpose to account for the book spine**
- Julian suggests Kindle Create App

## Researching Markets

**Best Seller Rank: 40K = selling 20 copies**
- Ideal BSR - below 200K
- **Use [JungleScout](https://www.junglescout.com/) to research books and markets to estimate sales**

**Assess competition: can you beat the bestseller in the category?**

**Use AMZ suggestion expander to see what people are actually searching for**
- E.g. for Debug Your Meditation - search Vipassana Meditation instead of just Meditation to reduce competition

Systematize your book publishing content - think of a system for your content and make tweaks to own the category with volume
- Use the same outline

Greg doesn't depend on ads much (he uses some)
- Automatic ads from Amazon
  - He spent $43.05 in the last30 days and earned $351.74 sales and ACOS was 12.24% (<50% = profit)
  - Free impressions (since it's cost-per-click only)

## How to Get Reviews

- **Write useful books**
- **Have a way for the user to contact you**
- **EMBED THROUGHOUT THE BOOK: Motivate users to contact you (freebie, etc. - 1:1 support, cheat sheet, etc.)**
