This is a list of exercises I did when preparing for my half marathon in 2018. They were very helpful! Most if not all are from Coach Sage Canaday and Sandy on Youtube.

- Heel kick
- Do marching in place and with skipping
- Do lunges
- Focus on push off and kicking back
- Practice running bare foot (but not all the time)
- Practice doing speed runs for form
- Do long runs of conversational pace
- Keep elbows in
- Work on the core

**Core exercises**

- Plank
- Side plank
- Bird dog
- Plank with elbows to hands
- Side plank with twist
- Stir the pot with exercise ball
- Banded Hip thrust
- Elbows to knees
- Mountain climbers
- Grasshoppers
- Hollow body
- Superman Bridge
- Sky reachers
- V ups
- Leg raises toes to bar
