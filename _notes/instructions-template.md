## How to add Buttons

1. Visit [indify.co](http://indify.co/)
2. Click on create button widget from their widget list
3. Add Button's Name, URL, and customize it
4. Copy the Embed link in below right corner of the sidebar
5. Now, paste that in your Notion Page

## How to Connect it to Custom domain

Please use Fruition for connecting the Landing page to your custom domain

[https://fruitionsite.com/](https://fruitionsite.com/)
