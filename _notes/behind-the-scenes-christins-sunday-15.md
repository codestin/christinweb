TBH I had a fraction of hesitation in showing you my Twitter stats, since this week had a conversation with a good “tech sage” friend about the **creator paradox:**

**What feels yucky:** Chasing stats. Gaming the algorithms so that people will see my stuff. Audience building *so* that you can sell them something later.

**What feels not yucky:** Creating for the sake of creating. Finding like-minded people to learn from. Share esoteric knowledge so they become more accessible.

**The *****Yucky Spectrum***** is part of the creator paradox because most activities have *****elements of both***. It’s a weird gray zone of being a sincere person making something, and being cunning about distributing the thing. The Woz and the Jobs, all in one package.

What are your thoughts on the **Yucky Spectrum**? What’s yucky vs not yucky to you?
