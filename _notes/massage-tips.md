**A list of tips from the Massage Sloth**

- Explore whole body, don't avoid the "jean shorts" area
- Lengthen body for better posture
- Massage pec area for better posture
- Shoulder stretch
- change up: tempo/angle/tool/position

**Esalen style**

- Match tempo of ocean waves
- Relaxation and getting in touch with body, not fixing problems
- Move per inhale/exhale of client
- Long/integrative strokes
