Forrest Prize Coins (FPCs) and [Forrest Prize NFTs](https://opensea.io/collection/forrest-prize-nfts) (FPNFTs) are given out by Forrest (and friends?) for exemplary literature.

FPCs replace the Booker Prize, because the Booker Prize sucks. - Forrest

## Potential applications:

- Grant FPCs to users, allow users to use FPCs to vote for their favorite books.
    - Author of the books receive FPCs → infer prestige
    - Limited number of FPCs per user → force rank favorites, incentivize more meaningful recommendations
- Users can grant each other FPCs for creative work → incentivize creativity
