## Narrative is credibility

One major insight I gained from my conversation with Chris is that I'm not wrestling with credibility, it's that I never truly shared my personal story across all the fields I'm working in now! The content I'm putting out right now, especially on Buddhism and science, can use more context as to the number of years I've invested in studying them, and the trials and tribulations it took to get here. With Write of Passage, I learned to share from the "now": what I know as of the moment I wrote them. Now it's time for me to share my past, to show how I got here with today's view points. The journey is its own credibility.
