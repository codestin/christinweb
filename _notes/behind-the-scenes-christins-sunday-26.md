Dear Ones,

The latest blip in my creator journey is reconciling my identities.

Back in October 2021, I went through Write of Passage, which led to a renaissance of projects, business ideas, writing, etc. that you have witnessed through this newsletter.

The journey felt like Kuòān Shīyuǎn's Ten Bulls painting, normally used to explain Zen enlightenment:

![](https://bucketeer-e05bbc84-baa3-437e-9518-adb32be77984.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/c24c4713-f330-46c5-8b0e-b3c771bedd2f_2644x1930.png)

[](https://substackcdn.com/image/fetch/$s_!5v5G!,f_auto,q_auto:good,fl_progressive:steep/https%3A%2F%2Fbucketeer-e05bbc84-baa3-437e-9518-adb32be77984.s3.amazonaws.com%2Fpublic%2Fimages%2Fc24c4713-f330-46c5-8b0e-b3c771bedd2f_2644x1930.png)Source: [https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ten_Bulls](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ten_Bulls)

But to interpret it in a *mundane, non-enlightenment-related* fashion, where I struggle with right now is **Step 10: Return to Society.**

By that I mean…I had **left Corporate Christin behind, in her sad lil’ corner.** She was the one who never felt like she fitted in. It was a constricted life, one where she never really got to/knew how to use what she was good at or enjoyed doing.

And Corporate Christin resides in [LinkedIn](http://linkedin.com/in/christinchong). I had avoided this social network, thinking that it’s like going back to the place where I never felt like I belonged. 

But what I didn’t realize was that **this avoidance is what’s leading to the identity fragmentation.** None of my friends or acquaintances in this world know about the “new” me. “New” in quotes, because it’s the “real” me.

So now that I have a firmer foundation with my writing and tweets, I’m going back in, baby! My friend Danny Naz recommended [The LinkedIn OS](https://theofficialjustin.gumroad.com/l/theos). (Since going back to the “old” world still gives me adverse visceral reactions, I’m “rewarding myself” through reliable dopamine hits from online courses…)

When I told this story to Write of Passage friends this morning, it seemed to resonate with other budding creators, as we transition from one identity to another. 

**What about you?****How do you handle online/offline identity transitions?**

Warm Wishes,

Christin
