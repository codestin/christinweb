My meditation teacher Gil Fronsdal often uses the term “reference point” to describe the purpose of mindfulness. The water in a clear mountain stream may seem placid and still, but if you place a stick in the water it will show that [the water is flowing](https://www.insightmeditationcenter.org/books-articles/how-mindfulness-works-when-not-working/). The stick is the reference point, and mindfulness practice is the ability to establish this stick during life, so we can see when our minds are fluttered. 

![](https://bucketeer-e05bbc84-baa3-437e-9518-adb32be77984.s3.amazonaws.com/public/images/c9616b5b-5869-45c4-8509-9a80c5b87830_4032x3024.jpeg)
