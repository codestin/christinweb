# Speaking for Companies

## My name is Christin Chong, PhD. I'm a neuroscientist and the author of Debug Your Meditation.

## I can help you and your team grow and thrive.

## What Your Team Needs

- Authentic Company Mission, Values, and Culture
- Mindful and Compassionate Leadership
- Employee Wellness and Engagement
- Diversity, Equity, and Inclusion

## These positive intentions are easy to name, yet hard to do. Why?

- Authenticity and wellness in the workplace are **rarely well-addressed** because they do not account for **unspoken misaligned goals**. This can happen between teams or individuals in an organization. More insidiously, it can happen our minds, between conscious and subconscious domains.
- I can help you and your team foster **genuine openness and clarity** that results from **goal-aligned** kindness and mindfulness practices.
