## What this site collects

- **Nothing personally identifying by default.** This is a static website with no accounts, no tracking pixels, and no ad networks.
- **Newsletter:** if you subscribe to the newsletter, your email address is collected and managed by Substack under [Substack's privacy policy](https://substack.com/privacy).
- **Hosting logs:** our hosting providers (Netlify, Cloudflare) keep standard, short-lived server logs (such as IP addresses) to operate and secure the site.
