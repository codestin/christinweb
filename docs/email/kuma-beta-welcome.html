KUMA · MEDITATION TIMER

Meet Kuma, your meditation timer.

Kuma is a gentle meditation timer for iPhone: choose your time, settle in, and let a quiet bell bring you back. The public beta is ready to try through Apple’s free TestFlight app.

  1. On your iPhone, install TestFlight from the App Store.
  2. Open the Kuma beta invitation.
  3. Tap Accept, then Install. Open Kuma and begin whenever you’re ready.

Install Kuma meditation timer ↗

Requires iPhone with iOS 18 or later. This is a beta, so you may encounter bugs. You can send feedback from Kuma’s Settings or through TestFlight.
